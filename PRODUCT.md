# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the request: Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, CSS/Tailwind where useful, Zod, Playwright, and Vercel. Avoid paid services and extra infrastructure for the initial release.

## Users

C3 community, institutional allies, and sponsors receiving an annual interactive recap; C3 staff prepare editorial content and private recipient editions.

## Product Purpose

C3 Rewind is an annual interactive thank-you and impact story. It shows what C3 did, credits those who made it possible, and invites continued collaboration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One shared presentation engine supports a public general edition and private editions reached by secret links.
- Editions are content-configured and can be added without duplicating the app.
- Individual edition links are bearer credentials. A recipient can forward a link, and anyone with it can view that edition.
- No database or paid service is required for the first release. Private configuration and generated PDFs are deployment assets; changing them requires a rebuild and deployment.
- No real private tokens, account credentials, DNS changes, or external deployment are authorized in this task.
- Editorial status values are pending, preliminary, validated, and approved for publication. Unverified facts, photos, and testimonials must not be invented.
- Initial indexing is disabled.
- Accessibility includes keyboard, touch, reduced motion, screen readers, and mobile safe areas.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve C3's navy foundation, Montserrat/Poppins typography and official logos; Compite is blue, Crea turquoise, Conecta purple, Copa uses its event colors, HTC uses orange and deep blue. Three animated orbits connect the narrative. Photography and individual slide compositions lead the experience.

## Product Principles

- Gratitude comes before promotion.
- Exact, sourced figures are accountability, not vanity metrics.
- The recipient is recognized in C3's work without overstating their contribution.
- The story keeps a user-controlled pace and remains readable without motion.
- Private access is enforced on the server for pages and PDFs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide semantic slide labels, visible focus, keyboard and touch navigation, reduced-motion support, contrast, responsive mobile compositions, safe-area spacing, and readable static PDFs.
