# C3 Rewind visual system

## Mode

Experience: one immersive story at a time; navigation and controls stay quiet while the slide composition leads.

## Visual authority

The latest supplied HTML is the visual and motion authority. The editorial v3 Google Doc is the narrative and content authority. The prior HTML is secondary and must not override them.

## System

- Navy canvas, layered scene-specific backgrounds, strong typography, real event photography, and varied compositions.
- C3 line colors: Compite blue, Crea turquoise, Conecta purple. Copa event accents are purple and turquoise; HTC accents are orange and deep navy.
- Three orbit rings persist as the narrative thread: they separate into the three lines, activate per chapter, and reunite in the close. A fourth orbit can identify a private recipient.
- Preserve official logos without distortion and use Montserrat/Poppins assets when provided. Keep text legible against scene backgrounds.
- Use circular reveals, image assemblies, counters, and controlled carousel motion only where the slide calls for them. Navigation remains user-controlled.
- The PDF state uses the same slide content and composition at a stable 16:9 frame; animations resolve to their final visual state.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop keeps horizontal scenes and the existing 16:9 stage. Mobile uses purpose-built compact layouts and internal slide scrolling where needed, not a vertically scrolling deck.

## Assets and editorial state

The source HTML's five embedded C3 marks are preserved as reusable assets. Pending photos are named placeholders only during development; production omits incomplete optional evidence and cannot publish an edition that fails its minimum-content checks.
